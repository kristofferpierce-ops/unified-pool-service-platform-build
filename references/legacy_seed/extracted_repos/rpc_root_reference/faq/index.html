Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ content helps the site rank for long-tail planning questions while making the booking process less confusing for first-time guests.

Where do your charters depart from?

Most departures are from Ocean's Edge Marina at 5950 Peninsular Ave Slip #612 in Key West. Confirmation details should be sent after booking.

Can we book online?

Yes. The current site uses FareHarbor for booking and availability. The redesigned site routes booking traffic through stronger internal pages first.

Are all trips private?

Most trips are positioned as private for your group. The charter hub also advertises a shared-seat Dry Tortugas option on select dates.

What should we bring?

Bring towels, sunscreen, drinks, food, and anything personal you want for the day. Trip-specific guidance can be confirmed after booking.

What if weather changes?

The team should confirm weather options before departure. Unsafe conditions should lead to a reschedule or the applicable refund option.

Can we customize the day?

Yes. The custom private charter page is designed for mixed-itinerary days.

Do you have a shared-seat option?

Yes. The current live charter hub advertises a shared Dry Tortugas trip.
