What to expect

Cruise west from Key West toward one of the most iconic national park destinations in the Florida Keys.

Explore Fort Jefferson, enjoy time in the water when conditions are favorable, and relax on a day designed around your group rather than a crowded ferry schedule.

Because the run is weather-sensitive, the best experience comes from setting expectations clearly and confirming details in advance.

Included and planning notes

The current live site says the charter includes the private boat, captain, cooler with ice, safety equipment, and snorkel gear when requested.

The live Dry Tortugas page also says food, drinks, gratuity, and fuel are handled separately, so keep that language consistent unless your booking flow changes.

Add a pre-trip confirmation email on the new host so guests know exactly where to arrive, what to bring, and how weather decisions are handled.

Why book private

A private trip gives your group more flexibility, more comfort, and more control over the pace of the day.

This is especially valuable for families, photographers, celebration groups, and travelers who want to pair sightseeing with time in the water.
