What this redesign fixes

  • Duplicate homepage H1 content and thin booking-page copy
  • Missing dedicated pages for sandbar, snorkeling, sunset, and custom charters
  • Weak internal linking caused by sending too much traffic directly to booking buttons
  • Missing FAQ structure, schema, and stronger local-service content

What the new host gets on day one

  • Responsive multi-page static site with preserved high-value slugs
  • Canonical tags, Open Graph tags, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, and JSON-LD schema
  • Booking page that supports direct conversion instead of acting like a dead-end stub
  • Image-ready gallery and social-preview assets that can be swapped once final photography is uploaded

Review themes already working for the brand

The live site already shows strong review signals around flexibility, local knowledge, and family-friendly experiences. The new site keeps those strengths but presents them with cleaner structure.

Comfortable boat, flexible day

Guests consistently describe the boat as comfortable and the experience as flexible, with routes and timing adjusted to match the group.

Strong local captain knowledge

Reviews repeatedly praise local knowledge, easygoing guidance, and smart plan changes when the weather shifts.

Great fit for families and special days

The current review themes include honeymoons, memorial outings, family groups, dolphin sightings, and custom snorkeling days.

Ready to move the site to a new host?
