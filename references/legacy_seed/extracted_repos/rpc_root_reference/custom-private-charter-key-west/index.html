How custom days sell

Custom-itinerary pages work best when they lower friction. Guests should feel like they do not need to know every detail before reaching out.

Frequently asked questions

Clean FAQ structure helps users and creates stronger page depth for search engines.

How many guests can come?

The live charter hub says the custom private charter can accommodate up to 12 guests.

Can we ask for multiple activities in one day?

Yes. This page is built around mixed-itinerary private charter requests.

Where do your charters depart from?

Most departures are from Ocean's Edge Marina at 5950 Peninsular Ave Slip #612 in Key West. Confirmation details should be sent after booking.

Can we book online?

Yes. The current site uses FareHarbor for booking and availability. The redesigned site routes booking traffic through stronger internal pages first.
